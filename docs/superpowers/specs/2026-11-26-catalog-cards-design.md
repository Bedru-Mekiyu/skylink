# Product and service pages: consistent cards (approved 2026-11-26)

**Intent:** product and service pages look modern, professional and consistent.
**Approach picked by the owner:** (1) refine the current brand (same blue, fonts, logo). Rejected: (2) a new visual style.

- One shared card on the product list and service category lists: 16:10 image, category, title, two-line description, up to 3 features, price, actions. The whole card opens the details.
- "View Details" is replaced by **Checkout** (adds the item, goes to the cart page). A cart-icon button adds and stays.
- Checkout again for a line already in the cart does not raise the quantity; a quantity chosen on a detail page replaces it.
- No visible fixed price, or priced per project: a single "Request a Quote" button.
- Prices read `ETB 60,000.00` everywhere, matching the cart and payment pages.
- Detail pages: same borders, radius and price style; each plan gets Checkout next to Add to Cart.
- Services landing page: category cards in the same style, whole card clickable; the top animation stays.

Non-goals: changing prices, categories, copy, admin, or payment rules.
